My Favorite Hobby

My favorite hobby is reading books. I love to read different types of books.

Why I Like It

I like reading because it helps me learn new things. Books take me to different worlds and teach me about life. Reading also improves my vocabulary and imagination.

How I Spend Time Doing It

I read books every day for one hour before going to sleep. On weekends, I spend more time reading. I usually read storybooks, science books, and magazines. I also visit the library every week to borrow new books.
